Tax assumptions

Uses 2026 federal long-term capital gain thresholds, a 3.8% NIIT threshold, and the latest FTB-published 2025 California rate schedule available in this build. California treats taxable capital gains as ordinary income. The app assumes the home was held long-term. Mortgage payments, property tax, and insurance affect net profit after ownership, not capital-gain basis. Net profit after ownership subtracts down payment and ownership costs from the cash left after the sale closes. Mortgage payoff is already subtracted in the cash-to-you formula. It does not handle depreciation recapture, partial exclusions, AMT, escrow prorations, SALT limits, or exact itemized deduction effects.
